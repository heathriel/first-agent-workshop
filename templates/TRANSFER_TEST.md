# Can you transfer this to your own work?

Seven minutes: five to design, two for a partner to challenge. Use the recurring task you chose at the start. A new participant should understand your plan without workshop vocabulary.

- User and decision: who needs what result, and what will they do with it?
- Input: give a sanitized example and its source.
- Choice: what uncertainty requires a lookup or judgment? If none, choose a script/workflow and explain why.
- Permission: name one allowed read/action and one withheld action.
- Output: sketch the smallest useful artifact.
- Test: give one passing case and one plausible-looking failure. Explain who/what checks each.
- Stop: give an attempt/time limit and the exact question sent to the human when blocked.

Partner: change one input, remove one fact, or contradict a source. Can the plan detect the problem without inventing an answer? Write the failure and revise one line of the plan. Save as `output/transfer.md`.

Exit ticket: explain the difference between your artifact check and your user's outcome check. A valid choice of a script is a successful result, too.
